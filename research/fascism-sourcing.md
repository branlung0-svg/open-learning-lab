# Fascism deep dive: sourcing pass (14 Nov 2026)

Output: `/tmp/work/fascism-sourcing.json` has 10 new event records, 11 replace or fix entries and 12 unresolved items. The JSON loads, and no text or cite field contains a straight apostrophe. I opened every page cited. No file under the repository was edited.

## Task 1: new records

- **Hunger Plan (fas-e-hunger-plan), Established.** Sources: Alex J. Kay's own essay (der Freitag, 2009) and the JSTOR Daily summary of his 2006 article in the Journal of Contemporary History. In late 1940 and early 1941 Backe proposed cutting Soviet food consumption to feed the Wehrmacht and Germany, and won Hitler and Göring over in January 1941. The 2 May 1941 meeting of state secretaries concluded that tens of millions would "undoubtedly" starve. The figure of about 30 million circulated among Nazi leaders. Kay links the plan to the siege of Leningrad (at least 800,000 dead, possibly up to 1.2 million) and to the deaths of Soviet prisoners of war. Those links could become a later record.
- **Forced labor (fas-e-forced-labor), Established.**
  - EVZ: more than 13 million people were forced to work in the Reich, another 13 million in occupied territories, about 26 million in all. The foundation paid 1.66 million people EUR 4.4 billion.
  - bpb (Pagenstecher, 2016): over 13 million in the Reich, and about 6 million foreign civilian forced laborers there in August 1944.
- **Rab/Kampor camp (fas-e-rab-camp), Established.**
  - The Memorial Museums portal gives about 15,000 people held on Rab, about 2,000 deaths and 1,433 victims named on the memorial plate. It says Italy refused to hand over about 3,500 Jews to Germany.
  - Detektor/BIRN gives 10,000 to 15,000 detainees. It quotes the historian Eric Gobetti for about 1,400 deaths and about 100,000 Yugoslavs interned in Fascist camps overall.
- **Ethiopia's claimed losses (fas-e-ethiopia-losses), Contested.** In 1946 Haile Selassie's government put 760,300 deaths to the Paris Peace Conference. Campbell judges the figure fair, and possibly too low. Bosworth urges caution about Campbell's reliance on late interviews. I found variant figures I could not reconcile; they are listed under unresolved.
- **1996 gas admission (fas-e-italy-gas-admission), Strong.** Pankhurst (1999) says it was "not until 1996" that the Defence Ministry admitted the gas use, and cites Del Boca's *I gas di Mussolini* (1996). This is one source only.
- **Blue Division (fas-e-blue-division), Established.** Moreno Juliá (2016) gives about 45,500 men rotated through the division and 5,000 dead. The division held sectors on the Volkhov and near Kolpino/Leningrad from October 1941 to October 1943, and the Blue Legion served until March 1944. Kirshner's review of Núñez Seixas gives 47,000 men. It reports Núñez Seixas's finding that Spanish troops did not take part in the killings of Jews, though antisemitic prejudice shows in their diaries. That finding could support a separate record.
- **Murayama statement (fas-e-murayama), Established.** Source: the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan's own text.
- **Kitterman (fas-e-kitterman-refusals), Strong.**
  - The 1988 article covers 85 cases. Kitterman found no proof that anyone was executed for refusing, and 49 of the 85 refusers suffered no negative consequences.
  - By 1995, the Deseret News reported 135 cases.
  - The copy I read is on gwern.net. It should be swapped for a licensed link before publication.
- **Who voted Nazi (fas-e-who-voted-nazi), Strong.** I read the authors' own PDF. It covers the elections from 1924 to 1933, using ecological inference. The Nazis' disproportionate gains came from the "working poor," especially in Protestant areas. The unemployed went to the KPD, and in some Protestant areas to the SPD. The Zentrum held many Catholic voters. Blue- and white-collar workers showed no consistent shift to the Nazis. The authors flag their uncertainty.
- **Election decimals.** I did not create new records, because the election records already exist. Instead I added sources to the five existing records:
  - The Bundestag Kalenderblatt gives 2.6 percent (12 seats), 18.3, 37.3 and 43.9.
  - DHM LeMO gives 33.1 percent for November 1932 and 43.9 for 1933.
- **"Trains on time" (fas-e-trains-on-time), Strong.** Stefano Maggi's 2023 railway-history conference paper:
  - It treats Ciano's 1924 slogan as propaganda.
  - It records more than 50,000 dismissals, which often targeted socialist and communist workers.
  - It gives no punctuality data, though it notes a 1928 Baedeker guide called trains generally punctual.
  - It shows that many improvements began before 1922.

  A BBC More or Less episode (21 Dec 2024, with Ruth Ben-Ghiat) is a second pointer; I read only its description.

## Task 2: Wikipedia replacements

- **Yekatit 12.** Sources are now the History Today review, the Hurst book page and Gelaye (2025). The text is reworded:
  - Campbell estimates about 19,000 dead, 19 to 20 percent of the city.
  - Pankhurst would put the toll nearer 10,000.
  - Some writers give up to 30,000.
  - The old figure of about 3,000 credited to Del Boca and Mockler is dropped, because I could not find a source for it.
- **Nanjing.** Britannica is kept, with two additions: Askew (2004, Asia-Pacific Journal), which surveys the Japanese historians' schools and explains why estimates differ, and Japan's Foreign Ministry, which says the killings cannot be denied. The text now names the estimators: China's official 300,000, Kasahara at 100,000 to nearly 200,000, and Hata at 38,000 to 42,000. Askew also describes an "illusion school" of denial. It is deliberately left out and not treated as a side.
- **Croatia (NDH).** Sources are now USHMM Jasenovac (320,000 to 340,000 Serbs and more than 30,000 Jews) and The Holocaust Explained (25,000 to 30,000 Roma, and race laws of 30 April 1941). The Holocaust Explained gives "over 350,000" Serbs, slightly higher than USHMM. The proposed text uses USHMM's figure.
- **Spain repression.** This record had no Wikipedia source, but it rested on one magazine review. I added The National's review of Preston (some 150,000 killed by the rebels; records destroyed) and the government's 2020 exhumation report: 785 graves opened and 9,698 people recovered between 2000 and 2019, and 2,457 graves mapped by 2017, a map the report calls incomplete. The text is unchanged.
- **Libya and Italy's racial laws.** The fact-checkers have already handled these. I offer optional additions:
  - Libya: the Lamma review (16 camps) and Ahmida's "two-thirds died" share, attributed to him. The unsourced 40,000 lower bound is dropped.
  - Racial laws: the CDEC exhibition guide (decree contents; the 1938 census counted 58,412 Jews).
